## Chapter 9 Conclusion: Consciousness Studies Returning to the Path of Materialism

This book proposes not an ultimate explanation, but a line of research:

- Starting from life (rather than information)
- Starting from structure (rather than functional description)
- Starting from metabolism and selection pressure (rather than subjective experience)
- Explaining consciousness through genesis rather than teleology

Consciousness is not a special gift of the universe, but a form of agency that naturally emerges in the process of life's complexification.

A true science of consciousness must return to this path of materialism.
