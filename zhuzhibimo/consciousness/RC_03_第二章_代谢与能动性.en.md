## Chapter 2: Metabolism, Material Constraints, and Survival—The Starting Point of Agency

The most fundamental difference between life and non-life lies in:

> **Life maintains metabolic order and resists entropy increase.**

Metabolism is not just the colloquial concept of "eating." On a physical level, it means:

- The continuous exchange of energy and matter through an open system
- Maintaining a local low-entropy structure under the thermodynamic constraints of global entropy increase
- This is precisely what Prigogine's theory of "dissipative structures" describes: an open system far from equilibrium maintaining order through energy dissipation

The existence of metabolism equips life with the most rudimentary **prototype of agency**:

- Distinguishing the inside from the outside (the establishment of boundaries)
- Maintaining boundaries (the stability of structure)
- Resisting dissolution (combating entropy increase)
- Actively acquiring resources in the environment (interaction with the environment)

Here, we propose our first assertion:

> **Agency originates first from metabolic structures, rather than a mysterious "mind."**
