## Chapter 7 Implications for Artificial Intelligence: Why "Pure Software" is Not Enough

If we accept that "consciousness = the structural expression of life's agency," then current AI (large language models), although they have passed the Turing test in symbolic processing, **do not possess consciousness** because they lack:

1. **Metabolic structure** (an internal mechanism to resist entropy increase)
2. **Survival pressure** (the real risk of dying if not recharged)
3. **Embodiment** (sustained interaction with the physical environment)

The implications of SATC for AI can be compressed into the distinction among three routes:

- **Representation and Language Route**: Acquiring strong representation capabilities through large-scale data and model structures, excelling in language, reasoning, and generation
- **Control and Embodiment Route**: Acquiring closed-loop capabilities with the environment through robotics, sensors, and control systems, excelling in action and adaptation
- **Self-maintenance and Synthesis Route**: Acquiring a sustained closed loop closer to that of life through energy/resource constraints and long-term self-maintenance mechanisms

The claim of SATC is not to deny the first route, but to remind us: even if it is extremely powerful, it does not automatically lead to Level III agency. What agency requires is structured closed-loop conditions, rather than merely stronger representation capabilities.

**The path to AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) or artificial consciousness must be the path of "artificial life"**: empowering systems with structural survival pressure to let "agency" emerge from the process of resisting entropy increase, rather than merely writing more complex algorithms.
