# $F_{damage}$ Dynamic Function: Complete Definition

## Core Proposition

$F_{damage}$ (price damage factor) is not a patch for "market failure," but a reconstruction of **price formation itself**.

In any transaction, the buyer and the seller each have a psychological anchor for the "acceptable exchange rate." In reality, however, neither party can escape a **synthetic, irresistible compound influence**—arising from the positioning of individuals by the intraspecific structure ($S_s$) and the rewriting of lifestyles by the level of tools ($T$). The price ultimately agreed upon is not the equilibrium outcome of free negotiation, but rather a "damaged price" under the influence of $F_{damage}$.

$F_{damage}$ measures: **the degree of deviation between the psychologically acceptable price of both transacting parties and the "ought-to-be price" in the absence of such synthetic influence**.

## Dynamic Function Formula

$$
F_{damage} = \frac{S_s \cdot T^{\alpha}}{1 - \beta \cdot e^{-\gamma(L - W)}}
$$

## Variable Definitions

| Symbol | Name | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| $F_{damage}$ | **Price Damage Factor** | A composite variable that exerts a synthetic influence on the psychologically acceptable price of both transacting parties. When $=0$, it represents equivalent exchange; when $>0$, the seller's price is depressed; when $<0$, structural compensation exists. |
| $S_s$ | **Intraspecific Structural Rigidity** | The degree of solidification of the organizational forms and distribution rules of intraspecific relationships. It manifests as class, institutional inertia, and power density. |
| $T$ | **Level of Tools** | The "metabolic level" at which a species extracts energy from the environment and converts it into survival advantages. **Tools themselves are neutral**. |
| $\alpha$ | **Acceleration Factor** | The non-linear amplification coefficient of tools on $F_{damage}$. When the rate of tool conveyance far exceeds the rate of structural optimization, $\alpha > 1$. |
| $\beta$ | **System Fragility Coefficient** | The sensitivity of the structure to survival crises, where $\beta \in (0,1)$. As $\beta \to 1$, the system fragility becomes extremely high. |
| $\gamma$ | **Tolerance Decay Coefficient** | The sensitivity of an individual's retention to the gap from the red line, where $\gamma > 0$. |
| $L$ | **Biological Survival Red Line** | The minimum threshold required for an individual to maintain basic survival. Below this line, the organism activates defense mechanisms (extinguishing of desires, behavioral stagnation). |
| $W$ | **Individual Actual Retention** | The net energy/resource retention actually obtained by an individual in a transaction that can be used to satisfy desires. |

## Three-State Analysis of the Denominator Term

- **$W \ll L$ (Retention far below the red line)**: The denominator $\to 1$, the individual is completely instrumentalized, and $F_{damage} \approx S_s \cdot T^{\alpha}$.
- **$W \to L$ (Retention approaching the red line)**: If $\beta \to 1$, the denominator $\to 0$, and $F_{damage} \to \infty$. **Singularity State**: Price signals collapse; no amount of money will make them do the job.
- **$W > L$ (Retention exceeding the red line)**: The denominator can become negative, and $F_{damage} < 0$. **Compensation State**: The individual has bargaining power and receives a welfare premium.

## Ought-to-be Price

$$
P_{natural} = \frac{P_{actual}}{1 - F_{damage}}
$$

- $0 < F_{damage} < 1$: The seller is depressed.
- $F_{damage} = 1$: **Market Failure State**, where the natural price approaches infinity.
- $F_{damage} > 1$: **Systemic Fracture State**, where the supplying party has physically withdrawn.
- $F_{damage} < 0$: A welfare premium exists.

## Hierarchical Decomposition of $S_s$

$$
S_s = S_s^{国家} \times S_s^{微观}
$$

- **$S_s^{国家}$**: The structural rigidity of the national political and economic system, which serves as the **meta-structure** for all microeconomic price formation.
- **$S_s^{微观}$**: The internal structures of lower-level organizations such as enterprises, families, and communities.

**Family-State Isomorphism**: $S_s^{国家}$ permeates downward, setting an upper limit on rigidity for all $S_s^{微观}$. The state is the "master valve" of $F_{damage}$.

## International Valuation Formula

$$
F_{damage}^{国际} = 1 - \frac{E_{PPP}}{E_{nominal}}
$$

The "valuation space" between the PPP exchange rate and the actual exchange rate is not a "market bias," but a **quantitative expression of the differences in $F_{damage}$ among countries**.

## Key Clarification Regarding $T$

$T$ (level of tools) is **neutral** in the formula. It is neither "good" nor "bad."

- In systems with extremely high $S_s$ rigidity, an increase in $T$ will only amplify $F_{damage}$ ($\alpha > 1$). Total Factor Productivity (TFP) grows, but welfare does not—the increment is intercepted by the structure. This is the "**Total Factor Productivity Illusion**."
- In systems with flexible $S_s$ (democratic checks and balances, welfare safety nets, trade union protection), an increase in $T$ may reduce $F_{damage}$ because institutions lift $W$ far above $L$.

**Tools always operate through humans. Technological progress itself does not automatically bring welfare.**
